### 🧠 Probabilistic Time-Series Research & State Estimation

Independent quantitative research into **latent-state estimation under noisy and incomplete observations**.

Developed a novel two-stage probabilistic architecture combining:

- Conditional normalising flows
- Causal WaveNet
- Innovation feedback
- Gated bias correction
- Autoregressive state estimation

The research investigated why prediction quality deteriorates across longer horizons and whether feedback mechanisms can reduce accumulating estimation error.

The resulting architecture **outperformed most comparison models under drifting conditions** and remained materially more stable over longer horizons.

A controlled ablation showed closed-loop training \(R^2\) falling from **0.506 to 0.062** when the principal feedback mechanism was removed.

The work also used **temporally later data and changing-condition tests** to investigate regime sensitivity, robustness and generalisation limitations.

`Python` `PyTorch` `Probability` `Time Series` `Normalising Flows` `WaveNet` `Model Validation`
